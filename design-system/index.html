Matter→Image→Light

A visual language for an auditory epidemic. Every infection is a drop of sound; the system gives each one a floor to land on, a pigment to carry, and a passage to move through.

01Principles

From the nave, the floor and the pigments
  1. The floor is the gridStructure is a fine light line laid on raw ground. Data is placed on it, never boxed in.
  2. Every case is a glyphOne mark per event, set precisely in relation to the others. Shape carries identity as well as colour.
  3. Colour is matterFive mineral pigments for lineages, nothing decorative. Everything else is concrete, graphite and quartz.
  4. One slit of lightA single accent marks what is active now. If two things glow, neither does.
  5. Symmetry along an axisCentre the wordmark and the main view on one vertical axis; let the rest settle around it.
  6. Day and nightTwo themes, each tuned on its own surface: washed concrete by day, deep blue passage by night.
  7. Light arrives and leavesMotion is slow change of light, not travel. Nothing bounces; under reduced motion, nothing moves.
  8. Raw and honestNo radii, no shadows, no gradients on controls. A line, a fill, a word.

05Motion

Light arrives and leaves

Light changes; objects don't travel. Use --ps-dur-slow for things appearing and fading, --ps-dur-fast for direct feedback on controls. Both drop to 0 under prefers-reduced-motion.
